# Pitch de featuring para Apple

Paso 68 (prep MAT-303). Se manda desde App Store Connect › Featuring Nominations (o el formulario "Promote your app" de developer.apple.com) **6 a 8 semanas antes** del lanzamiento, y otra vez con cada actualización grande. En inglés. Requiere la cuenta de Apple Developer (H1).

## Campos de la nominación

| Campo | Texto |
|---|---|
| Nomination type | New app launch |
| Name | Lumbre |
| Launch date | [fecha] |
| Countries of interest | Argentina, Mexico, Spain, Colombia, Chile, United States |
| Category | Productivity |

**Description** (qué es, en dos oraciones):

```
Lumbre is an idea-stacking app: you save what inspires you from anywhere on iPhone, and every morning two of your saved ideas from different domains connect into a "spark", a concrete new idea. It's built to make you think, not to store more notes.
```

**Why it's a great fit / What makes it unique:**

```
· One single, choreographed moment. The daily spark is revealed as a synapse firing: two neurons reach toward each other and light up, timed with haptics, in a full-screen modal over a blurred, Liquid Glass interface. With Reduce Motion on, it becomes a calm 200 ms fade.

· Designed for iOS 26. Native floating TabView with Liquid Glass that minimizes on scroll, a glass capture button, large titles, Dynamic Type up to the largest sizes, VoiceOver labels for every neuron and spark.

· Capture from everywhere. Share Extension, on-device dictation, VisionKit document scanning for book pages, a home screen widget and App Intents for Siri and Shortcuts. [Ajustar a lo que esté en el build.]

· Privacy by design. Files are deleted right after processing, there are no ads and no tracking, analytics never include user content, and the app asks for explicit consent before any content reaches the AI.

· Made in Argentina, Spanish first. Written in Rioplatense Spanish with full English localization, for creators and developers across Latin America.
```

**Story angle** (para editorial: por qué ahora, quién la hace):

```
Lumbre is made by a solo developer in Buenos Aires. It started from a simple frustration: we save hundreds of ideas and almost never come back to them. Instead of another place to store notes, Lumbre turns your saved ideas into new ones, one spark a day. It fits Apple's editorial themes around creativity, learning and mindful productivity, and the Spanish-speaking creator community in Latin America.
```
